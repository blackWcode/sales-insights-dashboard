01 · The project

Raw rows in, decisions out

This project shows a dynamic sales dashboard built with Excel and Power BI. It transforms raw monthly sales data into visual insights, so stakeholders can make informed decisions quickly and confidently.

The data is modelled with Power Query and DAX, and the report is designed so that non-technical users can read it at a glance, with interactive slicers for anyone who wants to dig deeper.

A portfolio exercise built on generated data, so it has no measured business impact.

02 · Process

Four steps from chaos to clarity

  1. 01

    Generate & store

    A sample dataset in Excel: 12 months × 4 regions × 4 products × 4 customer segments, with units, revenue, cost and profit.

  2. 02

    Shape with Power Query

    Load the workbook into Power BI and prepare the data for analysis.

  3. 03

    Model with DAX

    Measures for revenue, cost and profit that respond to every filter and slicer.

  4. 04

    Design the report

    Trends, breakdowns and slicers laid out using data visualisation best practices.
